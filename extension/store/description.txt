Second - Local AI Chess Coach
==============================

Tagline:
--------
Your local chess second.

Short Description:
------------------
Clear chess move explanations on your device. Active only after games end.

Detailed Description:
---------------------
Second is your local chess second in the Chrome side panel.
Standard engines show raw numbers like +1.4 or -2.8.
Second explains why moves are good or bad in plain English.

Key Features:
- Explain Move: Stream move commentary with tactical ideas, candidate lines, and alternatives.
- Longer Commentary: Generate a deeper explanation for the selected move.
- Local Stockfish + Optional Cloud Eval: Uses local Stockfish WebAssembly and optional Lichess Cloud Eval (on by default; turn off in Settings for offline position evaluation).
- On-Device AI Inference: Language models run locally in your browser via WebAssembly (wllama). Prompts and commentary stay on your device.
- Feedback Ratings: Save thumbs up or down ratings locally and export them as JSON.

Fair-Play Guarantee:
--------------------
Second is built only for study, learning, and post-game review.
Second works only after a game ends.
A fail-closed lock disables analysis during active live games, puzzles, and tactical modes.

Requirements:
-------------
- Google Chrome or a Chromium browser with Manifest V3 Side Panel support.
- ~1.2 GB of free disk space for Second 2B weights.
- A modern laptop or desktop computer.
