Variation 6A (Chosen Design) Center-Aligned Header
24... Nd4 · Blunder
Second 2B
Moving the knight to 24... Nd4 is a critical tactical mistake that surrenders the initiative.

While jumping into the center appears active, it abandons the defense of the f7 square. White refutes this immediately with 25. Bxd5!, unleashing an unstoppable double attack against the black king and the undefended rook on a8.
