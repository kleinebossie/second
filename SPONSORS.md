# Sponsors and Backers

Second is your local chess second.
It is free, private, and open-source under Apache 2.0 (and GPLv3 for the browser extension).
The extension runs local language models in WebAssembly (`wllama`) and local Stockfish in WebAssembly.
It includes an optional Lichess Cloud Eval toggle that you can turn off in Settings.
Second collects no telemetry and has no paywalls, subscriptions, or advertisements.

Sponsorships directly fund:
- **Cloud GPU Compute:** Training and evaluating model sizes (`Second-1.0-0.8B`, `Second-1.0-4B`, `Second-1.0-9B`) and Reinforcement Learning runs (GRPO / DAPO).
- **API and Evaluation Costs:** Automated judge runs and benchmark evaluations on our frozen 300-item test set.
- **Developer and Store Fees:** Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, domain registration, and hosting.
